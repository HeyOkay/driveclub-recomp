Environment timelapse (experimental)

Hold + to fast-forward the environment; hold - to rewind available session history.
Shift doubles the rate; Ctrl halves it. Release to return to normal speed.
Default rate: 600x. Car physics keep their normal clock; weather can change road grip.
Clouds, sky, weather, lightning, scene animation groups and shader wind share the visual timeline.
Rewind is limited to recorded history; particles are held during reverse playback.
Set BB_ENV_TIMELAPSE=0 before launch to disable the mod. Debug tracing is off by default.
